📖 Manager's Quick Guide

Kitchen Schedule v3.4 · PDF Attachment & Touch Edition

1. Card View (☰) vs. Grid View (⊞)

Use Card View to inspect a single day, check occupancy alerts, and edit an employee's details or FRY station. Switch to Grid View (⊞) to paint shifts across all 7 days with a pinned employee column.

2. Editing Shifts & Double Shifts

Tap any employee card to open the shift editor. Switch days at the top (S M T W T F S) without closing the modal. Tap + Double Shift to add a split shift (e.g., 5am-1pm / 4pm-11pm).

3. Managing Staff & Sections

Open your Account Menu (top left badge) → Manage Employees to add, edit, or remove staff. Tap ✏️ Sections on the tab bar to customize Kitchen, FOH, Banquet, or add new stations.

4. Occupancy Alerts & Daily Events

Tap the Occupied / Arrivals / Departures strip to enter daily hotel numbers (Occ % highlights in yellow above 65% and red above 85%). Days with events show a green dot on the day bar.

5. Email PDF Attachment & Printing

Tap 🖨️ in the top bar to generate a 1-page Letter Landscape PDF and either Email PDF directly as a file attachment or print it. Admins can check Hours & Overtime Report for >40h OT tracking and CSV export.
